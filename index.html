About Me

I am a technologist, educator and entrepreneur working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data science and human learning. My career has taken me through D. E. Shaw, Amazon and InMobi, where I worked on large-scale systems, recommendations and machine learning.

I founded CloudxLab, which has helped hundreds of thousands of learners develop skills in AI and data engineering. I am now building Terno AI, an enterprise-grade AI data scientist for secure, reliable analytics.

At the centre of my work is one question: how can we use technology not merely to make existing systems faster, but to help build a better human future?
