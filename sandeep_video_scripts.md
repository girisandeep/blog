## Script 1: Free AI & ML Course — Learn by Inventing

**Format:** Talking head
**Runtime:** ~2.5 minutes
**Purpose:** Announce free batch of AI & ML course, first session Sunday
**Event link:** https://cloudxlab.com/events/209/learn-by-inventing-agentic-ai-gen-ai/

---

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:20]**

Most people learn AI the wrong way.

They watch a lecture. They copy the code. They run the notebook. And then — two weeks later — they've forgotten almost everything.

I've taught hundreds of thousands of learners online. And I've seen this pattern again and again.

The problem is not the learner. The problem is the method.

---

**[THE PHILOSOPHY — 0:20–1:00]**

There is a different way to learn. I call it *Learning by Inventing*.

Instead of giving you the answer first, I give you a problem. A simple one. You think about it. You get a little stuck. And then — with the right nudge — you discover the idea yourself.

When you discover something, it becomes yours. You don't forget it, because you built it.

This is how I taught the Pythagorean theorem to a ten-year-old without ever stating it. I just asked her the right questions — and she figured it out herself.

That's the experience I want every learner to have with AI and machine learning.

---

**[THE ANNOUNCEMENT — 1:00–1:45]**

So here's what I'm doing.

I'm starting a new batch of my AI and Machine Learning course — taught entirely through this method. No passive video-watching. No copying code you don't understand. We build ideas from first principles, together.

The first session is **this Sunday**.

And here's the part I'm really excited about: **if you don't need the IIT certificate, the course is completely free.**

That's right. The full course. Free.

I'm doing this because I genuinely believe this method can change how people learn, and I want to prove it at scale.

---

**[CTA — 1:45–2:15]**

If you've ever felt like you're going through the motions in an online course — watching videos, passing quizzes, but not really *understanding* — this is for you.

If you want to actually think like an AI engineer, not just use one — this is for you.

The link to register is in the description. The first session is Sunday. It costs nothing to show up.

Come ready to think. Not to memorize.

I'll see you there.

---

**[OPTIONAL CLOSING LINE]**

*"I wrote a letter to H.C. Verma when I was in class eleven, from a small village in UP, with no reason to think he would ever reply. He did. That reply changed my life. I want this course to do something like that — for as many people as possible."*
